non-profit work) under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).

**Commercial use needs a separate written license.** This applies, for example, if you

- include the library in a product you sell or distribute for money (firmware, devices, apps),
- use it in a commercial service or in the internal tooling of a for-profit business,
- ship it as part of a paid library, framework or SDK.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask first - it costs nothing.

## How to get a commercial license

Write to **michi.fu@quickline.ch** with

1. your company / project name,
2. what the library is used for (product type, expected volume if it is sold),
3. the target hardware.

Terms are agreed individually and confirmed in writing.
